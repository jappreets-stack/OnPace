OnPace Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

OnPace is a step tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. In short: OnPace does not collect any of your data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, and no tracking.

What OnPace reads

With your permission, OnPace reads these from Apple Health on your device:

  • Steps
  • Walking + running distance
  • Finished workouts (to show them as sessions)

It also uses your phone's and watch's motion sensors to count your newest steps live (Apple Health saves steps every few minutes) and to tell when you're walking, so your character can walk with you. Motion data stays on your device. OnPace never writes to or changes your Apple Health data.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Everything stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Your iPhone and watch share your goal, character and today's numbers with each other directly, using Apple's built-in connection between them. OnPace has no servers and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Your settings

Your goal, character, style and sessions are saved only on your devices. Deleting OnPace deletes them. You can turn off OnPace's access to Apple Health at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

Children

OnPace does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
