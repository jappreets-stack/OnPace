## Version 1.0
**Promotional text (170 max)**

Know at a glance if you're on pace for today's step goal, and how many minutes of walking it takes to catch up. Your mini-me walks when you walk.

**Description**

OnPace answers one question at a glance: are you on pace for today's step goal?

• Your goal ring with today's steps and the real distance you walked
• On pace or behind, and exactly how many minutes of walking catch you up
• A forecast of what time you'll reach your goal at today's pace
• Pace that learns your usual day from your last 30 days
• Your streak and your last 7 days at a glance
• A mini-me you design (skin tone, hair, outfit, glasses and more) that walks when you walk and celebrates when you hit your goal
• Workouts you finish on Apple Watch show up with the steps you took during them
• Count the steps of anything: a hike, a dog walk, shopping, a theme park day
• Night red: the whole app and watch in red on black, easy on your eyes after dark. On Apple Watch it turns on by itself after sunset
• Three styles (Clean minimal, Cozy pastel, 16-bit adventure) and 8 color sets

APPLE WATCH
• The same ring and pace on your wrist, in your style
• Watch face complications for steps, steps left, and pace
• Numbers match Apple Health exactly

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. OnPace reads your steps from Apple Health, and everything stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

- **Notes:** OnPace has no account. It reads steps, walking distance and workouts from Apple Health (read only) and uses motion activity only to animate the character. Allow Health access on first launch; on a device with no Health data the ring starts at 0. The Apple Watch app and complications show the same numbers from Health on the watch.
